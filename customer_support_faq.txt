Returns & Exchanges
Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery when accompanied by the original receipt and packaging. Contact customer support through the website's contact form to initiate a return.

Shipping & Delivery
Standard shipping usually takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping usually takes 2–3 business days. Delivery times are estimates and may vary by destination.

Order Tracking
A tracking link is sent to the customer's email address once an order has shipped. The assistant cannot access live order information or provide real-time tracking updates.

Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled before they have been shipped. Contact customer support through the website's contact form as soon as possible to request cancellation.

Payment Methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards, and UPI payments.

Damaged Products
Report damaged products within 48 hours of delivery through the website's contact form and attach photographs of the damage.

Refund Processing
Approved refunds typically take 5–7 business days to appear in the original payment method, depending on the payment provider.

Contacting Support
Use the contact form on the website to reach the customer support team. The knowledge base does not specify a phone number, email address, or operating hours.
